7. AI Workflows & Tactics

2026-04-30 — Workflow: Build an AI cost-control operating model
Primary source: AI Daily Brief episode
The actionable theme is that agentic workflows can break flat-fee AI assumptions because long-running agents consume more inference, tool calls, and premium-model capacity.

What it does: Reduces AI spend leakage by routing work to the cheapest model that meets quality requirements, reserving expensive models for escalation cases.

Tools/models: Existing AI vendors in use across the organization; spreadsheet or BI dashboard for cost tracking; model bake-offs using candidate models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Microsoft/GitHub Copilot, or internal providers.

Setup steps:
1. Inventory recurring AI workflows by team, task, model, and monthly usage.
2. Run bake-offs on real tasks using cheap, mid-tier, and premium models.
3. Define escalation rules: cheap model first, stronger model only on failure, ambiguity, or high-value work.
4. Track cost per completed task, not just token cost.
5. Assign an owner to maintain routing rules and vendor comparisons.
6. Review weekly for runaway agent loops, unused subscriptions, and premium-model overuse.

Available now: Yes.
Why it matters: The near-term enterprise advantage is not just model access; it is disciplined model routing and cost observability.
