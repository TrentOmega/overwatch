9. Podcast Highlights

  • 2026-08-19 — AI Daily Brief: AI engineering skills map for knowledge workers
    Link: YouTube
    Summary: The episode frames knowledge work as shifting from executing tasks directly to managing AI agents that execute those tasks. It identifies five practical skills: capability mapping, context and harness management, problem/product prototyping, opportunity identification, and rapid skill acquisition.
    Available now: Yes; these are workflow practices rather than a new product dependency.
    How to use it:
  • Map recurring work into tasks where AI can draft, search, transform, test, or monitor.
  • Define the harness: inputs, tools, files, constraints, examples, and acceptance criteria.
  • Prototype the product or process quickly, then refine using observed failure modes.
  • Track repeated friction as automation opportunities.
  • Treat new tools as skills to learn rapidly through small production tasks. Why it matters: The durable advantage is not just model access; it is the ability to structure work so agents can operate with context, constraints, and verifiable outputs.
